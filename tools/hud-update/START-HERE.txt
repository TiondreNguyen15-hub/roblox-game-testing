HUD READER UPDATE

1. Leave your CURRENT working demo open so the updater finds it.
2. Extract ALL files from the ZIP. Do not run inside the ZIP.
3. Double-click Update-HUD.cmd. It closes and reopens your current app.
4. Open HUD Reader > Open recording. Choose HUD-Sample.mp4 from the app
   folder for a first test, or choose your own MP4 gameplay recording.
5. Pause the clip, drag a box around only the ammo digits, choose Ammo
   counter, and press Read current frame. Or enable continuous HUD reading.
6. Save HUD setup to keep your region. Export OCR CSV saves actual readings.

Your runtime/gpu and existing settings are NOT replaced. No GPU reinstall.
Windows OCR reads the text on CPU; the CUDA model benchmark stays separate.
Parsed counters/banners are candidates, not guaranteed gameplay facts.
No trained Apex detector or automatic aiming is added.
See HUD-README.md and HUD-VALIDATION.md in your app folder after updating.
